**Solis Heat** — a complete open-world crime game in vanilla JavaScript: no libraries, no assets, no network. Explore the sun-soaked city of Marina Solis (3600×2700, 7 avenues × 5 streets, beach and sea, ~40 named buildings, 9 landmarks) on foot or behind the wheel of six original car models. Steal parked cars, dodge 13 traffic cars and 26 pedestrians, and manage a heat-based wanted system (0–170, 34 per star): police Interceptors pursue, ram, set up bust pressure on foot, and open fire at three stars. Work for fixer Tía Carmen through a four-mission story — a first delivery, a timed run under a two-star ambush, a pawn-shop robbery and escape, and a final neon heat run — then take generated side jobs for cash and respect. Full HUD with live minimap, mouse-aim pistol combat, BUSTED and TAKEN DOWN outcomes, localStorage saves, and synthesized WebAudio engine and siren sounds. Everything is original: city, characters, vehicles and story are invented for this entry.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser — or just open `game/index.html` directly. Keyboard and mouse: WASD/arrows to move, F/E to enter and exit cars, mouse to aim, click to shoot, K to save.
